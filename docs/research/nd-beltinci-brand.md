# ND Beltinci — brand research

Accessed 10 September 2026. This research records public design facts for a local, non-official prototype; generated events, capacities, sales and revenue are explicitly demo data.

| Source | Observation | Confidence | Prototype decision |
|---|---|---:|---|
| [ND Beltinci club site](https://www.ndbeltinci.net/) | Club/entity name is ND Beltinci; first team is often presented as Beltinci Klima Tratnjek. The site uses *Beltinčani* and *žuti*. | High | Use ND Beltinci as tenant name and natural Slovenian supporter copy. |
| [Official derby ticket announcement](https://www.ndbeltinci.net/clanek/1007/predprodaja-vstopnic-za-prekmurski-derbi-med-beltinci-klima-tratnjek-in-muro-v-1-krogu-pokala-pivovarna-union.html) | Historical 2 Sep 2026 Pokal Pivovarna Union derby listed tribuna adult €15, youth (up to 15) €5; standing adult €10, youth free. It calls Mura match a *prekmurski derbi* and recommends early arrival. | High | Preserve categories/prices; use a future **DEMO** fixture date so no stale fixture is shown as live. |
| [Official fan shop](https://www.ndbeltinci.net/fan-shop) | Yellow, light blue and dark blue club merchandise; 2025/26 season ticket listing shows €120 (existing holders €110). | High for colors/value on cited page | Yellow primary, navy contrast and sky utility token. Use €120 as a clearly demo season-pass display. |
| [NZS stadium profile](https://www.nzs.si/klubi/moski/2-skl-vzhod/klubi/nd-beltinci/stadion) | Venue is Športni park Beltinci; profile describes natural grass and a covered stand. | High | Use verified venue name; do not present seed allocations as stadium capacity. |

## Derived theme

The public sources establish yellow and blue as club-facing colors, not exact official hexadecimal specifications. The tokens `#F5C400`, `#092B59`, and `#5EA9DD` are research-derived prototype approximations, centrally kept in `src/web/style.css`. The app uses a local shield-shaped “NDB” monogram rather than copying or hotlinking the club crest. A visible “Demonstracijski sistem” indicator avoids implied official endorsement.
